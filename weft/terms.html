weft terms of service

weft is a group-planning messaging application operated by Phil Cunliffe. It sends automated text messages to the registered members of a private group. By opting in you agree to these terms.

Program description

weft sends transactional messages about your group's plans: invitations, notices that a group chat has been opened, reminders to answer, and post-event wrap-ups. It does not send marketing.

Consent

Your group's organizer adds your number with your agreement. weft then sends one confirmation text, and you receive further messages only after replying YES. Only confirmed members are messaged.

Message frequency

Varies with the group's plans, typically a few messages a week and more around an event.

Msg & data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. Reply START to rejoin.
