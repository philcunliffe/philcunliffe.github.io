weft privacy policy

weft is a private text-message assistant used by one small group of friends to coordinate plans. It is operated by Phil Cunliffe and is not offered to the public.

What is collected

Your name, your mobile number, the messages you exchange with weft, and the plans and replies those messages describe. Nothing else.

How it is used

Only to run the group's plans: asking who is free, opening a group chat for an event, and remembering what the group decided. Messages are processed by an AI model to compose replies.

Who can see it

Only the people who were in the conversation where something was said. A message sent privately to weft is never shared with anyone else unless you ask. Every stored fact records who was allowed to see it, and you can text "what do you know about me" at any time to see everything weft holds about you and who else can see it.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Messages are carried by Twilio and a messaging relay, and replies are generated by Anthropic's Claude, each acting only as a processor for this service.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while you are in the group. Text STOP to leave; your number is removed from the allowlist immediately. To have your stored messages and facts deleted, contact Phil at phillip.cunliffe@gmail.com.
