weft

weft is a group-planning messaging application operated by Phil Cunliffe. It texts the members of a private group so they can coordinate events: who is free, a group chat for the plan, a reminder to answer, and a wrap-up afterwards. Membership is by invitation from the group's organizer.

How to join

Text START to +1 (425) 696-4833 to opt in to weft messages.

By texting START you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from weft about your group's plans at the number you text from. Message frequency varies, typically a few messages a week and more around an event. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. You will receive messages only if the group's organizer has added your number.

What happens next

After you text START you will receive one confirmation: "weft: You're in. weft will only text you about your group's plans, a few messages a week and more around an event. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help." From then on weft texts you only about your group's plans.

Leaving

Reply STOP to any message. You will get one confirmation and nothing further. Reply START to rejoin.
